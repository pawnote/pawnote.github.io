How it works

From your words to a link, in three steps

It takes a few minutes and works on a phone.

Choose a design

Open a design from the home page. Each one shows its price, the regular price and how much you save.

Add your words

Fill in their first name, your name, the letter and your question. The preview beside the form updates as you type, and you can play it before paying.

Pay and send

Pay once with UPI, card or net banking. You get a private link right away. Copy it and send it.

What the other person sees

They open your link and the character walks in with your letter. They read it, turn the page, and answer your question. Every no gets a sadder reaction and another chance. A yes ends with a happy celebration.

Good to know

  • Your whole letter lives inside the link, so there is nothing to log in to.
  • Paying covers the exact letter you wrote. You can copy its link again any time on the same device.
  • If you change the words, you create a new link.
  • Links work on any modern phone or computer.
