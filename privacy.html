Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026

What we collect

Before you pay we ask for your name, email address and mobile number. We use them to send your receipt, to help if something goes wrong, and, only if you tick the box, to tell you when new designs launch. You do not need an account. The words you write (names, letter and question) are packed into the link you create. After you pay, we also keep a copy of your letter and its link together with your order details, so we can restore your link if you lose it and help if something goes wrong. We never show letters in any public place.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to support you and meet our accounting duties. To have your letter and details deleted, write to support with your order ID.

Who can read your letter

Anyone who has the link can read the letter, so share it only with the person you mean it for. If you lose the link, write the letter again.

Heart QR code

The heart QR code is drawn on your own device from your link. We do not upload it anywhere.

Our server

Orders are created and checked on our own secure server, which also saves your order details in our private order sheet. Your payment keys never reach your browser.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay shares with us a payment ID, the amount, and the names you typed on the letter so we can help you if something goes wrong.

On your device

Your draft and the letters you have paid for are remembered in your own browser so you can come back to them. Clearing your browser data removes them.
