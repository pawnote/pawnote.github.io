Refund policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

When we refund

  • You were charged but did not receive a working link.
  • You were charged more than once for the same letter.
  • The letter does not play correctly and we cannot fix it.

When we cannot

Because each letter is a digital product that is delivered instantly, we cannot refund a letter that works as described just because you changed your mind.

How to ask

Write to support within 7 days of paying. Include your order ID (starts with PN-) or your Razorpay payment ID. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 7 working days.
