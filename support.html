Common questions

How does the price work?

Each design has a one-time price. You pay once for a letter and get a private link. There are no subscriptions and no account to create. The regular price and your saving are shown on every card.

How do I send it?

After you pay, you get a link that contains your whole letter. Copy it and send it by message, email or anywhere else. It opens on any phone or computer with nothing to install.

Does the other person need an account?

No. They open the link and the story plays straight away.

Can I change a word after I have paid?

Paying covers the exact letter you wrote, and you can copy its link again any time. If you change the words you create a new link, which is a new purchase.

Is my letter private?

Yes. Your letter is stored inside the link itself, not in a public gallery. Only people you send the link to can read it. See the privacy page for details.

What if something goes wrong?

Write to us on the support page. If you were charged and the link did not work, we will fix it or refund you. The refund policy has the details.
